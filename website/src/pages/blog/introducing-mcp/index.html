The proposed OFFPEAK API makes the spending boundary explicit. An application discovers a model, inspects a quote, authorizes a cap, and submits the request. This guide illustrates that flow without inventing a live endpoint or SDK.

Explore the planned request flow

The example stages below describe the intended pilot. They use pseudocode and cannot submit a paid request. The initial scope supports non-streaming text and EOA wallet authorization; direct x402 payments and delegated permissions are later work.

    A quote is planned to expire after 30 seconds. A changed prompt requires a fresh quote. Capacity and funds are not reserved yet.

    Know the boundary

    Accept only while the quote and capacity are valid. An unexpired quote may still encounter unavailable capacity.
