OFFPEAK sells open-model AI inference from real, bonded GPUs at prices that drop when those GPUs are idle, settled in USDG on Robinhood Chain.

Why it matters

  • Real compute, not resold credits. Every model is open-weight and served by a registered, bonded host. OFFPEAK doesn't proxy any third-party API account.
  • The discount comes from idle time. Hosts post 168 hourly prices, and router-measured load can cut a quiet host's live price to 0.4× its posted price.
  • Your balance is cash. Prepaid USDG sits in a vault that you can withdraw from. Withdraw-all completes within 2 hours.
  • Agents pay as they go. Payment channels and x402 let an agent pay per call with no account.
  • Proof, not promises. Every response carries a usage record signed by the router and the host, and hourly totals go on chain. Hosts bond USDG and are audited for model swaps.

What $OFPK does

$OFPK is a utility token. Its hooks switch on after written legal review.

  • Fee tiers. Lock $OFPK to lower the 4% OFFPEAK fee by up to 1 percentage point.
  • Early Window access. First access to Windows, OFFPEAK's tradable capacity reservations.
  • Host bond weight. $OFPK bonded by a host ranks it higher in routing, capped at +25%, and is slashable.
  • Burn. 5% of fees buys and burns $OFPK.

Holding $OFPK alone earns nothing. It is not a share of fees, a yield or an investment.
