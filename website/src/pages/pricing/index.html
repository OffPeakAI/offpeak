What sets the actual rate?

The proposed pricing engine responds to occupied serving slots, subject to a measured cost floor. The production model, settlement asset, benchmarked rates, and paid service are still being validated.

Can I withdraw unused funds?

The design returns available, unreserved paid funds to the same wallet, token, and network. OFFPEAK controls the prepaid treasury and processes withdrawals. The preview does not accept deposits.
