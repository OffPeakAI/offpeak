PH-00Operating contractPlanned

Define the pilot's custody, token units, authorization, finality, and receipt semantics before dependent interfaces are built.

Evidence
Decision records, a field dictionary, deterministic signing and receipt fixtures, and named external dependencies.
Gate
No undefined monetary units or trustless-execution claims; every requirement has an assigned phase and evidence gate.
PH-01Scaffold and staging baselinePlanned

Establish a reproducible application, private staging environment, pinned dependencies, database, and redacted logging.

Evidence
A lockfile, local database setup, build and verification commands, staging health output, and a deployment runbook.
Gate
A clean checkout passes CI and staging health checks. Readiness fails correctly when its database is unavailable.
PH-02Wallet identity and signingPlanned

Establish account ownership and explicit inference or withdrawal intent with wallet signatures.

Evidence
Independently checked EIP-712 fixtures, owner-scoped reads, session handling, and replay and tampering tests.
Gate
Valid authorization passes; altered, expired, replayed, wrong-network, and wrong-purpose signatures fail. A session cannot authorize payment.
PH-03Ledger and reservationsPlanned

Keep an immutable balanced journal and reserve available funds atomically before any work is dispatched.

Evidence
Exact integer arithmetic, unique business events, recovery fixtures, and concurrent reservation tests against a real database.
Gate
No imbalance, negative available balance, or duplicate charge under races, repeated events, and transaction-boundary crashes.
PH-04Real GPU and quote enginePlanned

Serve a pinned, licensed model on authorized hardware and derive provisional price floors from actual serving economics.

Evidence
Worker identity and capacity data, exact model manifests, reproducible workload benchmarks, metering, and a measured cost floor.
Gate
Real hardware meets the frozen quality threshold. Pricing respects floor rates, quote expiry, stale capacity, and full worker slots.
PH-05Finalized depositsPlanned

Credit the ledger once from verified, finalized token transfers. Validate the intended production asset separately from mock test funds.

Evidence
Token identity and transfer behavior, finality semantics, restartable event indexing, testnet transactions, and reconciliation.
Gate
Duplicates and reorganized events cannot inflate balances. A finalized testnet deposit credits once; a transaction hash alone is insufficient.
PH-06Paid inference loopPlanned

Connect quote acceptance, signed intent, funds and capacity reservation, execution, result recovery, and exact charging.

Evidence
A real-worker test-funded request, durable encrypted results, the supplied charge fixture, and crash and retry recovery transcripts.
Gate
A request is executed and charged once within its cap. Retries recover the same result, and a refunded request cannot receive a late charge.
PH-07WithdrawalsPlanned

Return unused funds to the owning wallet with an atomic hold, durable signed-transaction record, and recovery-safe broadcasting.

Evidence
Finalized testnet withdrawal records plus tests for races, lost RPC responses, transaction replacement, reverts, and reorganization.
Gate
Each intent results in exactly one transfer or an explicitly unresolved hold, with reserve coverage preserved and no foreign destination accepted.
PH-08Receipt registry and verifierPlanned

Publish immutable receipt commitments and provide a verifier that can validate a full bundle independently of the OFFPEAK API.

Evidence
A minimal testnet registry, canonical receipt and Merkle fixtures, publisher controls, finalized bundles, and tampering tests.
Gate
A genuine bundle verifies; altered data fails; historic roots cannot be overwritten. Registry payloads contain no customer content or wallet addresses.
PH-09Customer console and agent clientPlanned

Make the complete testnet journey usable, with real domain states, a runnable client example, and local receipt validation.

Evidence
A clean-environment quickstart and browser checks for expired quotes, insufficient funds, wrong networks, failed workers, and pending proofs.
Gate
A new user completes the testnet journey. Every route has real data or an honest empty state, supports keyboard navigation, and contains no secrets.
PH-10Operations and hardeningPlanned

Demonstrate that operational failures preserve funds and privacy, and complete focused review of payment and custody controls.

Evidence
Role restrictions, audit trails, reconciliation, retention jobs, alerts, incident drills, and an isolated backup restore reconciled forward.
Gate
Recovery and retention checks pass, with no unresolved critical or high-severity payment or custody defect before real-money operation.
PH-11Capped deployment and pilotPlanned

Deploy a reviewed release for a capped, allowlisted paid pilot after supply, custody, jurisdiction, asset, and signer dependencies are resolved.

Evidence
Production deposit, inference, receipt, and withdrawal records; a verified deployment manifest; and a supervised pilot report.
Gate
All acceptance criteria pass, customer liabilities remain fully backed, workload quality meets its threshold, and measured paid work has positive contribution margin. Elapsed time alone is not a pass.
